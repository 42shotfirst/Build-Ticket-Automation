DATA FLOW OVERVIEW

         ↓
         ComprehensiveExcelExtractor
         ↓

         ↓

         Terraform Data Structure

             ├── terraform.tfvars (variable values)
             ├── outputs.tf (output definitions)
             └── provider.tf (Azure provider config)
